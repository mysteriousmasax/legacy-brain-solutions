You have significant rights concerning your personal data, including the right to:

6. Tanzanian Regulatory Compliance

Legacy operates in strict adherence to the regulatory frameworks of the United Republic of Tanzania. This includes full compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act and the ethical and professional standards set forth by the National Board of Accountants and Auditors (NBAA). Our data handling procedures are regularly audited to ensure ongoing compliance with these national mandates.
